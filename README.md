## 🎮 Gameplay Features
- **Clueless Crossword + Wordle Deduction:** Tap any Across or Down word slot in the 5×5 or 6×6 crossword grid to load it into the active 5-guess Wordle panel below.
- **The Cascade Hook 🔒:** When you solve a word, it locks into the crossword grid. Every intersecting letter shared with unsolved perpendicular words automatically pre-fills as a locked **Green Hint**!
- **Hints, Free Letters & Give Up 💡:**
  - **3-Word Hint:** Shows 3 candidate words that fit your current clues (including the exact answer)—tap one to load it right into your guess row!
  - **Free Letter:** Permanently unlocks an unrevealed letter you haven't already guessed Green (prioritizing intersections to help two words at once).
  - **Give Up:** Voluntarily reveal a tough word for 0 points (`+1 Strike`) while still unlocking its intersecting letters so you can always finish the board.
- **Steep Fewer-Tries Scoring & Endless Hint Rewards:** Earn massive points for solving words on your 1st or 2nd try, plus earn **bonus Hints in Endless Mode** on 1st-try solves, Cascade auto-solves, and cleared boards!
- **3 Difficulty Levels:**
  - **Easy:** `5×5 Grid • 6 Words • 1 Starter Letter • 3 Hints`
  - **Medium:** `6×6 Grid • 6–7 Words • Classic Deduction • 2 Hints (1.25x Points)`
  - **Hard:** `6×6 Dense Grid • 7–8 Words • 1.5x Points • 1 Hint`
- **Expanded 14,300+ Offline Dictionary:** Curated 3- to 6-letter English vocabulary including singular & plural nouns, names of people, places, and everyday objects—100% offline with zero network dependencies.
